🚀 Thrilled to unveil my latest project for the #JuaraVibeCoding challenge: Luminair — The Smart Pulse of Your Environment. 🌬️✨

Luminair isn’t just another weather dashboard; it’s a multi-sensory experience that fuses atmospheric science, multimodal vision, and generative art. I wanted to build something that doesn't just show you data, but lets you "see" the air around you and dynamically adapt your indoor spaces.

Key Features:
💨 Real-Time Air Quality: Deep-dive into pollutants (PM2.5, NO2, CO) with real-time tracking and 5-day predictive trends.
🎨 Generative Visuals: An HTML5 Canvas engine that morphs in real-time based on local weather—from serene clear skies to turbulent storms.
🗺️ Interactive Global Map: Tap anywhere on the planet to instantly scan the atmospheric health of that location.
🤖 Gemini Health Advisor: Deeply personalized health recommendations and outdoor safe-windows, tailored to your demographic profile using Google’s latest Gemini model.
📷 HVAC Filter Health Check (Multimodal Vision): Upload a photo of your AC or heating filter—Gemini instantly checks dust congestion, estimates remaining lifespan, and calculates energy drag to keep your airflow pristine.
🌿 Biophilic Indoor Plant Advisor (Natural Purification & AI Image Editing): Upload a room photo—Gemini identifies plant species, evaluates biophilic well-being, and suggests NASA-certified air-purifying foliage.
🪄 AI-Powered Room Editing: Don't just analyze; co-create! Ask Gemini to edit your room image (e.g., "Add a snake plant in the corner") in real-time, view it in a premium full-resolution lightbox with custom prompt captions, and instantly analyze the new organic air-purification score of your edited space.

The Tech Stack:
🔹 Frontend: Pure Vanilla JavaScript & CSS for maximum performance, with a sleek glassmorphic UI and animated official Google Gemini brand integration.
🔹 Backend: Containerized with Docker and served via Nginx.
🔹 Cloud: Deployed to Google Cloud Run.
🔹 DevOps: Infrastructure-as-Code with Terraform and a fully automated CI/CD pipeline via GitHub Actions and Docker Hub.

Building this was a masterclass in "Vibe Coding"—balancing technical precision with creative aesthetic.
