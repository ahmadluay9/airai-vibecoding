🚀 Thrilled to unveil my latest project for the #JuaraVibeCoding challenge: Luminair — The Smart Pulse of Your Environment. 🌬️✨

Luminair isn’t just another weather dashboard; it’s a multi-sensory experience that fuses atmospheric science with generative art. I wanted to build something that doesn't just show you data, but lets you "see" the air around you.

Key Features:
💨 Real-Time Air Quality: Deep-dive into pollutants (PM2.5, NO2, CO) with real-time tracking and 5-day predictive trends.
🎨 Generative Visuals: An HTML5 Canvas engine that morphs in real-time based on local weather—from serene clear skies to turbulent storms.
🤖 Gemini Health Advisor: Deeply personalized health recommendations and outdoor safe-windows, tailored to your demographic profile using Google’s latest Gemini 3.5 model.
🗺️ Interactive Global Map: Tap anywhere on the planet to instantly scan the atmospheric health of that location.

The Tech Stack:
🔹 Frontend: Pure Vanilla JavaScript & CSS for maximum performance and a sleek glassmorphic UI.
🔹 Backend: Containerized with Docker and served via Nginx.
🔹 Cloud: Deployed to Google Cloud Run.
🔹 DevOps: Infrastructure-as-Code with Terraform and a fully automated CI/CD pipeline via GitHub Actions and Docker Hub.

Building this was a masterclass in "Vibe Coding"—balancing technical precision with creative aesthetic.
